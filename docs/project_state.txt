CANDIDATE GENERATION RULES

Leadership candidate generation MUST use:
- Upcoming licensing vendors
- Upcoming consumption vendors
- Upcoming Microsoft vendors
- Upcoming new spend vendors

Candidate generation MUST NOT use:
- Licensing cost-out-to-date
- Consumption cost-out-to-date
- Microsoft cost-out-to-date

Rationale:
Cost-out-to-date items are already completed or committed.

Leadership attention should focus on active opportunities and unresolved future positions.

Completed opportunities are intentionally excluded.

RISK GENERATION RULES

Leadership risks must remain grounded in:
- commentary
- movement data
- current financial positions

Not:
- assumptions
- predictions
- invented concerns

Risks should identify:
- blocked decisions
- stalled progress
- unresolved actions
- deterioration
- savings exposure
- leadership dependencies

Financial watchouts should identify:
- cost increases
- savings erosion
- forecast deterioration
- renewal increases
- budget exposure

Leadership watchouts and financial watchouts must remain separate concepts.

LEADERSHIP INSIGHT RULES

Leadership insights are intentionally separated from Risks & Watchouts.

Reason:
Insights explain broad themes. Watchouts explain concerns. They serve different executive purposes.
This separation is approved and should not be collapsed.

Leadership insights explain: emerging patterns, concentration of savings, areas requiring awareness, leadership-level implications. They are not risk statements.

WATCHOUT RULES

Executive watchouts should focus on:
- leadership intervention
- escalation
- blocked decisions 
- stalled progress 
- action dependency 

Financial watchouts should focus on:
- budget deterioration
- negative costout
- renewal increases
- forecast deterioration
- savings erosion
- financial exposure

Executive watchouts and financial watchouts are distinct concepts.

Validation is ongoing but separation is approved.

CURRENT APPROVED WATCHOUT BEHAVIOUR

Executive watchout: One primary executive concern
Financial watchout: One primary financial concern
Additional financial risks: Display beneath primary watchout

Financially material items must never be suppressed because a larger financial issue exists.
