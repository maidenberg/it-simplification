(1) PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Build an AI-powered executive communications engine for IT Simplification.

Business deliverable:
One weekly leadership email to Matt Toohey and Lewis Lee.

Nothing else is a business deliverable. All other files are supporting, intermediate, diagnostic, validation, traceability, or aggregation artefacts. Artefact rationalisation has removed several previously-maintained outputs and future artefacts should only be retained where they directly improve the weekly leadership email.

The governing question for all future work is: "Does this improve the quality of the weekly leadership email?"

If the answer is no, the work is outside the active project scope.

The email must remain concise, evidence-based, grounded in source data and commentary, and useful to Matt Toohey and Lewis Lee.

The system should surface the handful of matters that genuinely belong in Ari's weekly email, including where supported by evidence:
- material week-on-week movement
- significant cost-out improvement
- significant cost-out deterioration
- material financial exposure
- contracts requiring leadership attention
- blocked decisions
- stalled progress on material items
- leadership dependencies
- risks to the FY27 position

The project is not a general executive communications platform. 

The project is not intended to create multiple stakeholder products, executive briefing products, monthly reporting products, presentation content, or communication variants unless a future stakeholder request demonstrates that such work directly improves the weekly leadership email.

The project briefly drifted towards a broader "executive communications engine" with stakeholder-specific outputs and executive briefing artefacts. That direction has been formally retired. The active scope is limited to improving the weekly leadership email to Matt Toohey and Lewis Lee.


(2) PRIMARY BUSINESS OUTCOME

Produce a high-quality first-draft weekly IT Simplification leadership email requiring less than five minutes of Ari's review and editing before distribution.

The email must explain, concisely and without invented narrative:
- what changed
- what improved
- what deteriorated
- what needs attention
- why leadership should care
- the relevant financial position where material

Avoid:
- unsupported interpretation
- invented executive storytelling
- generic management language
- filler text
- long narrative expansion
- speculative explanations
- special-case code where a one-off business exception can be handled through Ari's review

Prefer:

- vendor or business issue
- source commentary
- financial exposure where material
- clear differentiation between leadership intervention and financial exposure
- plain-text readability

Executive usefulness is more important than verbosity.

Formatting is not the primary challenge, but it must not be discounted. A correct signal that is difficult to scan, visually noisy, contradictory, or rendered like debugging output still reduces the quality of the weekly email.

Formatting work is justified when it demonstrably improves:
- clarity
- scanability
- plain-text readability
- Outlook compatibility
- forwarding behaviour
- the ability to understand the important matters within approximately 30 seconds

(4) PROJECT PHILOSOPHY

The following principles are mandatory.

1. Reuse existing components. 

Do not rebuild extraction, comparison, ranking, scoring, commentary enrichment, candidate generation, theme classification. These are considered complete.

2. Reuse existing outputs. 

Always consume existing reporting outputs, existing movement analysis, existing commentary, existing risk generation. Executive layers should be built on top of them rather than replacing them.

3. Turn data into judgement.

The purpose of executive outputs is not restating spreadsheet values or repeating commentary. 

The purpose is explaining significance, surfacing priorities, highlighting intervention points, and identifying leadership-relevant risks, while remaining grounded in existing reporting outputs.

4. Grounding rule 

Executive narrative must remain dashboard grounded, commentary grounded, and reporting-output grounded. Never introduce unsupported conclusions.

(9) REPORTING RULES

1. Mandatory metric definitions

Net identified costout = signed total costout across all tracked categories.
Finalised positive costout = positive costout where Finalised = Y
Unfinalised positive costout = positive opportunities not yet confirmed
Budget variance / reclassification = absolute value of negative costout movement
Entered renewal price = populated renewal price values
Gross positive costout = positive costout only

2. Commentary rules

All commentary must:
- be source-supported
- reference explicit spreadsheet values
- reference explicit status notes
- remain traceable

Never invent causes, decisions, nor future outcomes.

3. Savings rules

Must distinguish finalised and unfinalised savings. Never combine them. 

Missing renewal prices must never be treated as savings.

4. Risk rules

Risks must surface:

- negative movements
- deteriorations
- missing values
- repeated stagnation
- unresolved approvals
- unresolved discussions
- unresolved renewals
- anomalies
- reclassifications


(10) LEADERSHIP COMMUNICATION RULES

1. Executive watchouts

Intended for intervention, escalation, and decision support.

Executive watchouts should represent:
- blocked progress
- leadership involvement required
- unresolved dependencies
- escalation candidates

Approved watchout behaviour:
- One primary executive concern where a qualifying executive concern exists.
- One primary financial concern where a qualifying financial concern exists.
- Additional material financial risks displayed beneath the primary financial watchout.
- Financially material items must never be suppressed merely because a larger financial item exists.

A single weak commentary phrase such as "No progress" does not automatically justify executive promotion. Repeated stagnation may be more meaningful, but no new repeated-stagnation code rule has been approved.

2. Financial watchouts

Should focus on:
- savings exposure
- renewal deterioration
- cost increases
- forecast impact
- budget pressure

Financial watchouts are distinct from executive watchouts.

3. Leadership insights

Leadership insights remain separate from risks. 

Do not merge.

Current architectural decision: Leadership Insights ≠ Risks & Watchouts

(14) APPROVED JUDGEMENT VALIDATION FRAMEWORK

Status: APPROVED

The following tests and PASS criteria are the project's formal judgement-validation framework:

1. Executive Watchout
PASS when the item requires leadership attention, intervention, escalation, decision, or action.

2. Financial Watchout
PASS when the item represents material financial exposure, deterioration, or savings risk.

3. Leadership Insight
PASS when the insight clearly explains why leadership should care.

4. Risk
PASS when the risk is actionable and supported by dashboard commentary.

5. Promotion Logic
PASS when material items are surfaced and are not suppressed by ranking or promotion behaviour.

6. Commentary Traceability
PASS when commentary can be traced to dashboard notes and source values.

7. Executive Usefulness
PASS when Lewis can identify the most important issues within approximately 30 seconds.

The framework distinguishes technical correctness from business usefulness.

(16) VALIDATION FINDINGS

Finding 1
Executive watchout severity may be overstated.

Observed example:
AWS Marketplace - Cloudec
Commentary:
"No progress"

Conclusion:
A single instance of stagnation does not necessarily justify executive attention. Repeated stagnation may be more meaningful, but no new rule has been approved. 

Status: NOT CURRENTLY OPEN.

Finding 2
Silverleaf is a special business exception, not a risk requiring code remediation.

Facts:
- Silverleaf Solutions Australia Pty Ltd works only on the Microsoft EA renewal.
- The $200,000 position is represented through the Silverleaf advisory line.
- Lewis understands who Silverleaf is and the relationship to the Microsoft EA renewal.
- Silverleaf would not necessarily appear in Ari's final edited email.
- Silverleaf itself is not a risk.

Decision:
Do not change the Python logic solely for this exception. The pipeline is working correctly. Handle the exception through Ari's normal review where needed.

Status:
CLOSED AS BUSINESS EXCEPTION, NOT A BUG.

Finding 3
Executive and financial watchouts are distinct.

Approved distinction:
Executive watchout → leadership intervention, escalation, decision, blocked progress, or action dependency.
Financial watchout → financial exposure, deterioration, renewal increase, savings erosion, or forecast impact.

Status:
APPROVED through the formal validation framework.

Finding 4
"Items to monitor" may imply leadership ownership.

The wording can suggest that Matt or Lewis should take action when many items remain operational.

No general wording change has been approved.

Treat this as a presentation consideration during email review, not an active logic defect.

Finding 5
Financial context improves leadership usefulness.

A commentary-only signal such as "Still under discussion" may not explain why leadership should care.

Preferred concise content where evidence exists:
- vendor or business issue
- source commentary
- material financial exposure

Avoid unsupported business significance statements.

Finding 6
Material financial deterioration was previously under-promoted.

Observed example:
MightyHive disappeared from promoted leadership outputs despite a material negative cost-out position and active commentary.

This finding led to BUG-001.

Status:
RESOLVED through multi-financial-risk promotion.

Finding 7
BUG-001 root cause was single-winner-per-theme behaviour.

Code pattern:

    by_theme[theme] = candidate

Effect:
Silverleaf became the sole financial_risk winner and other material financial risks were suppressed.

Status:
RESOLVED.
