VALIDATION FINDINGS

Finding 1

Executive watchout severity may be overstated.

Observed example:

AWS Marketplace - Cloudec

Commentary:

"No progress"

A single occurrence does not necessarily justify executive attention.

Repeated stagnation over multiple snapshots appears more meaningful.

Finding 2

Financial watchout communicates the wrong entity.

Current output:

Silverleaf Solutions Australia Pty Ltd
Approximately $200,000 of additional cost is currently reflected in the position.

Issue:

Silverleaf is the advisory vendor.

The issue leadership actually cares about is the Microsoft EA financial position.

Technically correct.

Potentially not the most useful executive communication.

Finding 3

Executive watchout and financial watchout are not yet clearly differentiated.

Validation suggests:

Executive watchout

- leadership intervention required
- blocked decision
- stalled progress
- escalation required

Financial watchout

- savings exposure
- budget deterioration
- renewal cost increase
- forecast impact

Current distinction requires validation.


Finding 4

"Items to monitor" may imply leadership ownership.

Observed reaction:

The wording suggests Lewis or Matt should take action.

In many cases ownership remains operational.

Validation finding only.

No approved change.


Finding 5

Outputs lack financial context.

Example:

Voucherify
Still under discussion

does not answer:

Why should I care?

Future judgement improvements may need:

- costout impact
- financial implication
- business significance

while remaining grounded in existing reporting outputs.


Finding 6

Financially significant deterioration may be under-promoted.

Observed:

24-8 → 31-8 comparison

MightyHive disappeared from leadership outputs.

Current live position:

Budget ≈ $83.4k
Renewal ≈ $120k
Negative costout ≈ $36.6k

Commentary:

Matt asked business to review usage
Business working on it
Document with Matt for signature - $ may not be final

Validation concern:

The engine may currently favour commentary keywords over material financial movement.

Finding 7 

BUG-001 root cause identified. 

Observed: Financially material deteriorations were correctly: 
- generated 
- ranked 
- classified but only one financial_risk candidate was promoted. 

Root cause: Single-winner-per-theme behaviour. 

Code pattern: if theme not in by_theme: by_theme[theme] = candidate 

Result: Silverleaf became the sole financial_risk winner. Other material financial risks were suppressed. 

Finding 8 

Financial context significantly improves executive usefulness. 

Observed: Vendor-only promotion provided insufficient context. 

Example: Ahrefs Pte Ltd Required: 
- financial exposure 
- commentary (if present) 
Leadership usefulness improved after financial context rendering. 

Finding 9 
Brevity appears preferable to narrative expansion. 

Observed: Short executive signals appear more useful than longer explanatory prose. 

Direction: 

Retain: 
- vendor 
- commentary 
- financial exposure 

Avoid: 
- lengthy executive narrative 
- generic executive filler text 
- unsupported interpretation Validation with Lewis and Matt pending.
